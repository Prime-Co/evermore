About Evermore

Built for the AI-powered earning economy.

Evermore AI Platform (Ever AI) brings AI tools, digital opportunities, training tasks, surveys and eligible remote work together in one place — so everyday people can earn on their own terms, at their own pace.

Evermore AI is a global AI-powered earning platform serving Nigeria and Africa. We connect our members to real, verifiable income streams — rating EverAI responses, correcting EverAI's memory, completing opinion and survey tasks, and accessing eligible remote work opportunities surfaced by EverAI from around the internet.

The primary objective of the Evermore AI Platform is to help people earn steady income online and move toward financial freedom — on their own terms, and at their own pace. By centralising access to AI training tasks, remote work opportunities, a skills Academy, and a personal Telegram agent, Evermore makes earning from home simple, transparent and sustainable.

Getting started is straightforward. Complete your Evermore registration and activate your Evermore Premium Subscription — one plan, full access for a whole year. Once activated, your personal Telegram agent is assigned, and you can begin earning from any of six categories: Rate EverAI Responses, Correct EverAI Memory, Opinion & Survey Tasks, Audio Transcription, AI Content Rating and Click n Earn — with combined earning potential up to $19.2/hr. Rewards are paid three times a week.

Already registered? Sign in to your Evermore dashboard to track tasks, monitor earnings, and manage payouts effortlessly, 24/7.
