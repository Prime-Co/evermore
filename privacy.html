How Evermore collects, uses and protects your data.

Last updated: June 20, 2026

Evermore User Privacy Summary

At Evermore, handling system processes with maximum transaction privacy is fundamental to our operations. This policy explains our data management rules across your Evermore registration, active task metrics, and daily dashboard processing. We secure user details to keep your digital account experience stable and safe.

1. Information We Collect

We safely gather specific profile data you provide directly during setup, including your full legal name, active email, telephone contact number, country, and your optional referral code. We also track task activity — completed EverAI ratings, corrected memory entries, opinion and survey responses, audio transcription, AI content rating, and click n earn activity — to correctly process your rewards.

2. How We Use Your Information

Collected data points are used exclusively to secure your user profile, maintain platform features, process task rewards, verify payouts across currencies, prevent network abuse, respond to support queries, and improve overall features across the Evermore AI Platform.

3. Data Security & Encryption Standards

We deploy industry-standard database encryption to safeguard your balances and profile from modern exploits. However, security is a shared responsibility. Members must maintain the confidentiality of their personal Evermore login details and must never expose active passwords to third-party agents — including anyone impersonating Evermore.

4. Sharing of Profile Information

Evermore does not sell, rent, or lease your private data to external marketing platforms. Information is only processed through secure cloud infrastructure providers and verified payment processors (such as our Squad checkout), or when required by authorized regional laws.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Account Management

Members can request access to, correction of, or permanent erasure of their stored data at any time. To modify your personal data or request account deletion, please submit an official ticket through our customer support portal, or message your personal Evermore agent on Telegram.

6. Cookies & Analytics Trackers

Evermore uses lightweight browser cookies and session keys to optimise mobile navigation speeds, maintain dashboard sessions, and analyse visitor behaviour across the site. You can modify your browser privacy settings to drop or block these tracking keys — though some parts of the dashboard may not work correctly without them.

7. Periodic Privacy Updates

We reserve the right to modify this policy to ensure ongoing security and compliance. Important updates will be highlighted via notices inside your user dashboard or sent directly to your registered email address.

Have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data? Please reach out via our Customer Support Portal.
