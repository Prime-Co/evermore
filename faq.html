Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Evermore.

Answers to the most common questions about registration, activation, earnings and payouts on the Evermore AI Platform.

Q.What is Evermore?

Evermore is an AI-powered platform that brings digital opportunities, surveys and eligible remote work together in one place — across six earning categories: rating EverAI responses, correcting EverAI's memory, opinion & survey tasks, audio transcription, AI content rating and click n earn. Evermore also offers an Academy for high-income skills and a personal Telegram agent for every activated member.

Q.How do I register on Evermore?

Click Create account anywhere on the site, fill in your full name, username, phone, country, email and password through the secure Evermore registration pipeline. Once submitted, you'll be taken to your dashboard where you can complete your activation.

Q.How do I activate my account?

After registering, click ACTIVATE ACCOUNT on your dashboard and complete the secure ₦15,000 payment for the Evermore Premium Subscription through our Squad checkout. Our team verifies your payment and approves your activation — you'll then be assigned a personal Evermore agent on Telegram to begin.

Q.What subscription is available?

Evermore now has exactly one subscription: the Evermore Premium Subscription — ₦15,000 (≈ $10) for 1 year / 12 months. It unlocks the full Evermore experience: priority access to all six earning categories, social media management opportunities, graphic design and copywriting gigs, virtual assistant and AI survey tasks, a personal Evermore agent on Telegram, and priority support.

Q.How do I earn on Evermore?

EverAI is continuously improved through human interaction and feedback. Activated members earn by rating EverAI responses (up to $16.2/hr), correcting EverAI's memory (up to $18.6/hr) and completing opinion and survey tasks (up to $17.2/hr). EverAI also scans the internet regularly for remote opportunities — audio transcription, AI content rating, click n earn and survey and opinion tasks — with combined earning potential up to $19.2/hr. Earned rewards are paid three times a week.

Q.Can I withdraw in my preferred currency?

Yes. To fully support our international users across regional zones, the system tracks, accumulates and routes withdrawals straight to your preferred local bank account in your native currency denomination — NGN, USD, GHS, KES, XAF or EUR.

Q.Is Evermore safe?

Absolutely. Evermore is engineered on top of a resilient, enterprise-grade global architecture. To guarantee maximum safety, always use our official portals via the Evermore sign in page, complete payments only through our secure Squad checkout, and review our Scam Alert guide to avoid off-platform bad actors.
